# Kweneng International Secondary School Management System

## User Manual

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** 30 July 2026  
**Audience:** School administrators, staff, teachers, students, and parents

---

## 1. About this manual

This manual explains how to use the Kweneng International Secondary School Management System (KISS Management System), including the web portal and the Android parent and teacher applications.

The options visible to you depend on your account role and the responsibilities assigned by the school. If an option described here is not visible, confirm that you are signed in with the correct account and contact the system administrator if necessary.

### Roles covered

| Role | Main responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Administrator | System setup, users, academics, records, reports, communication, and oversight |
| Teacher | Marks, attendance, punctuality, behaviour, homework, schemes, timetable, and requisitions |
| Headmaster | Academic oversight, comments, reports, marks monitoring, events, and teacher functions |
| Student | Dashboard, timetable, marks, report information, and library records |
| Parent/Guardian | Children’s records, marks, reports, homework, timetable, library, communication, and documents |
| Accounts Officer | Fee imports, fee-access controls, reports, and exam summaries |
| Librarian | Books, copies, loans, returns, and overdue items |
| Office | Student profiles, events, notices, messages, reports, and exam summaries |
| Register Officer | Attendance-register monitoring, exports, and events |
| Inventory Officer | Stock items and requisition processing |

> **Important:** Screens and data vary according to the current academic year, active term, class assignments, and account permissions.

## 2. Getting started

### 2.1 What you need

- A current web browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
- The school portal address supplied by Kweneng International Secondary School.
- An active school account and password.
- Internet access.
- For mobile use, an Android device with the relevant Kweneng application installed.

### 2.2 Sign in to the web portal

1. Open the school portal.
2. Select **Log in**.
3. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
4. Optionally select **Remember me** only on a private device.
5. Select **Log in**.
6. You are sent to the dashboard for your role.

Never save your password on a shared or public computer.

### 2.3 First sign-in and temporary passwords

If the school issued a temporary password, the system may require you to create a new password before continuing.

1. Enter the temporary credentials.
2. On the password-change screen, enter the requested current or temporary password.
3. Enter and confirm a new password.
4. Save the change and use the new password for future sign-ins.

Choose a password of at least eight characters. A longer, unique passphrase is recommended.

### 2.4 Reset a forgotten password

1. On the login screen, select **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter the email address registered with the school.
3. Submit the request.
4. Open the password-reset email and follow its link.
5. Enter and confirm a new password.

If the email does not arrive, check the spam or junk folder and confirm that the school has your correct email address.

### 2.5 Change your password while signed in

1. Open the account menu in the top navigation.
2. Select **Change Password**.
3. Enter the requested password details.
4. Save the new password.

### 2.6 Sign out

1. Open the account menu.
2. Select **Log out**.
3. Close the browser when using a shared device.

## 3. Parent registration

Parents may create an account with the 10-character parent code printed on a child’s school login slip. The code is valid for seven days and can be used only as permitted by the system.

1. On the portal login page, select **Register as a parent**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
3. Optionally enter your home address.
4. Select your **Relationship to Child**.
5. Enter the 10-character **Parent Code** from the child’s login slip.
6. Enter and confirm a password of at least eight characters.
7. Select **Create Account**.

After successful registration, the account is linked to the child and the parent dashboard opens.

If the code is invalid, expired, or already used, ask the school to verify the link or issue a new login slip. If you already have a parent account, sign in with the existing email; contact the school when another child must be linked.

## 4. Using the web portal

### 4.1 Navigation

- Use the dashboard cards or menu links to open a module.
- Use breadcrumbs, where shown, to return to a previous section.
- On a small screen, open the menu button to display navigation options.
- Use filters before opening, printing, or exporting large reports.
- Select the school logo or **Home** to return to the public website; use your dashboard link to return to the secure portal.

### 4.2 Forms and messages

- Fields marked as required must be completed.
- Validation messages explain missing or invalid information.
- A success message confirms a completed action.
- A warning or error means the change was not completed; correct the indicated information and try again.
- Avoid selecting **Save**, **Import**, or **Submit** repeatedly while a request is processing.

### 4.3 Search, filters, and exports

Most list pages allow one or more of the following:

- Search by a person, class, subject, title, or reference.
- Filter by academic year, term, class, status, or date.
- Open a record for more details.
- Print the current view.
- Download PDF or CSV output.

Choose the correct filters before exporting. A downloaded report reflects the selected academic year, term, class, or student.

### 4.4 Dark mode and accessibility

Where available, use the appearance control to switch between light and dark display modes. Browser zoom and operating-system accessibility settings can also enlarge text. Do not rely on colour alone when interpreting a status; read the accompanying label.

## 5. Administrator guide

The administrator configures the system and controls most school records. Complete academic setup in the order shown below before expecting marks, reports, or timetables to work correctly.

### 5.1 Recommended initial setup order

1. Create or activate the **Academic Year**.
2. Create the year’s **Terms** and activate the current term.
3. Create **Classes**.
4. Create or import **Students** and assign them to classes.
5. Create **Teachers** and other staff accounts.
6. Create **Subjects**.
7. Assign subjects to classes.
8. Assign teachers to class subjects.
9. Assign individual students to the appropriate subjects where required.
10. Create departments and assign Heads of Department where applicable.
11. Configure and publish the timetable.

### 5.2 Manage user accounts

Open **Users** to manage staff accounts across supported roles.

To create a user:

1. Select **Create User**.
2. Enter the person’s details.
3. Choose the correct role.
4. Enter or generate the initial password as provided by the form.
5. Save the account.
6. Give credentials to the user through an approved secure channel.

From the user list, an administrator can edit an account, activate or deactivate access, and reset a password.

Deactivation prevents access without deleting historical records. Prefer deactivation when a staff member leaves or an account is temporarily suspended.

### 5.3 Manage students

Open **Students** to create, view, edit, and remove student records.

For each student, verify:

- Name and contact details.
- Admission or identity information.
- Current class.
- Parent or guardian links.
- Emergency contacts.
- Account status.

Use **Reset Password** when a student cannot access the portal. Use **Login Slip** to print the student’s login details and parent registration code. Bulk slips can be generated for selected students.

Bulk deletion is permanent and should be used only for records entered in error. Confirm the selection carefully and preserve required school records.

### 5.4 Manage classes

Open **Classes** to create and maintain class groups.

1. Create a class with its required name and details.
2. Open the class to view enrolled students.
3. Add or assign students through the available student workflow.
4. Remove individual or selected students only when they no longer belong to the class.

Removing a student from a class is different from deleting the student’s account.

### 5.5 Manage staff and parents

Dedicated sections are available for **Teachers**, **Parents**, **Librarians**, and **Accounts Officers**. Use these pages for role-specific profiles. General staff roles can also be managed from **Users**.

Before creating a duplicate parent, search by email and confirm whether the existing parent can be linked to another child.

### 5.6 Academic years and terms

Open **Academic Years** to create, activate, close, or lock a school year. Only one academic year should be active.

Open **Terms** to create term dates and control term status.

- **Activate** makes a term the current working term.
- **Lock Midterm/Endterm** prevents changes to the corresponding results.
- **Finalize** marks the term’s work as complete.
- **Lock** prevents further changes.
- **Unlock** is an exceptional administrative action and should follow school approval.

Before finalizing or locking, confirm that marks, comments, attendance, and reports are complete. Locked records may no longer be editable by teachers.

### 5.7 Subjects and teaching assignments

Open **Subjects** to create and edit subjects.

Use **Manage Classes** to assign subjects to class groups, then use **Manage Teachers** to assign teachers. Student-subject assignments can be maintained under **Student Subjects**, including preview-and-apply import workflows.

Check assignments carefully because they determine:

- Which classes and subjects a teacher can see.
- Which students appear on marks and homework screens.
- Which subjects appear on student reports.

### 5.8 Departments and schemes oversight

Open **Departments** to create departments and assign members or Heads of Department.

Open **Schemes** to review submitted schemes of work:

1. Filter or select a scheme.
2. Review the plan, topics, subtopics, and progress.
3. Select **Approve** when it meets requirements.
4. Select **Request Changes** and provide clear feedback when revision is needed.

### 5.9 Timetable administration

Open **Timetable** to configure the timetable.

1. Create a timetable template.
2. Add days and periods.
3. Create rooms and teaching groups as needed.
4. Add entries by selecting the day/period, class or group, subject, teacher, and room.
5. Resolve any clashes shown by the system.
6. Configure cycle anchors if the school uses a rotating cycle.
7. Review the complete timetable.
8. Select **Publish** when it is ready for staff, students, and parents.

Publishing makes the chosen template the live timetable. Review it before publishing because users depend on the active version.

### 5.10 Marks and results

Open **Marks** to search, view, edit, or remove mark entries where permitted.

For spreadsheet imports:

1. Select the required academic year, term, class, and assessment context.
2. Upload the file.
3. Review the import preview.
4. Correct rejected or unmatched rows.
5. Apply the import only when the preview is correct.

Use **Exam Summaries** to select filters, preview the summary, and download PDF output.

Use **Reports** to find a student, preview the report card, download a student PDF, or generate a bulk PDF for the selected group.

### 5.11 Promotions

Open **Promotions** to move students into the next class or academic year.

1. Select the source year/class and destination.
2. Review eligible students.
3. Promote an individual student or use bulk promotion.
4. Confirm the result before leaving the page.

Use **Reverse Promotion** only to correct an incorrect promotion. Verify both the student and the destination because class history and reporting depend on these records.

### 5.12 Events and announcements

Use **Events** to create, update, and manage calendar items. Add the title, dates, description, and other required details. Comments may be reviewed or removed where authorised.

Use **Announcements** to publish notices to a selected audience:

1. Create the announcement.
2. Enter the message and choose its audience.
3. Confirm its visibility or active period.
4. Publish.
5. Open **Tracking** to review delivery/read activity.
6. Export tracking to CSV or send a reminder where appropriate.

Choose the audience carefully to avoid exposing information to unintended recipients.

### 5.13 Parent messages and absence notices

Open **Messages** to review parent conversations. Open a thread, read its history, and submit a reply.

Open **Absence Notices** to review notices submitted by parents. A notice can be marked as seen and later resolved after the school has processed it.

### 5.14 Documents, alumni, and activity logs

- **Documents:** Upload school documents, control whether they are active, and remove obsolete files. Active documents can be downloaded by parents.
- **Alumni:** Manage alumni records and expressions of interest. An interest can be processed or converted into an alumni record.
- **Activity Logs:** Search or filter recorded system activity for operational review and accountability.

## 6. Teacher guide

Teachers see only classes and subjects assigned to them. Headmasters can also access teacher functions.

### 6.1 Dashboard and timetable

The dashboard summarises teaching assignments and provides shortcuts. Open **Timetable** to view the published teacher schedule. If it is empty, confirm that the administrator has published a timetable and assigned you correctly.

### 6.2 Enter marks

1. Open **Marks**.
2. Select the academic year and term.
3. Select the assigned class and subject.
4. Select the assessment type or marks context.
5. Load the students.
6. Enter each mark, observing the allowed range.
7. Save the marks.
8. Review the confirmation and correct any validation errors.

Existing marks can be opened and edited while the relevant results period is unlocked. Use the print option for a printable class result view.

For an import, select the correct context, upload the supported file, and review all errors before completing the import.

### 6.3 Take attendance

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Select the class and date.
3. Mark each learner with the appropriate attendance status.
4. Add notes where required.
5. Save the register.

Use **Summary** to review attendance. Print, PDF, and CSV options are available for authorised reporting. If a parent submitted an absence notice, check the linked information before completing the register.

### 6.4 Record punctuality

1. Open **Punctuality**.
2. Select the class, learner, and date.
3. Enter the required late/early or timing information.
4. Add a note if needed.
5. Save.

### 6.5 Record behaviour

1. Open **Behaviour**.
2. Select the learner and relevant class/context.
3. Choose or enter the behaviour category.
4. Enter the date, details, and action taken where requested.
5. Save the record.

Use factual, professional language because behaviour records may be reviewed by school leadership.

### 6.6 Assign and mark homework

1. Open **Homework**.
2. Select the class and subject.
3. Enter a title, instructions, and due date.
4. Attach a supported file or image if needed.
5. Enable homework mark tracking if the task will be graded.
6. Save or send the homework.

To enter homework marks, open the homework item, select **Marks**, enter the results, and save. Attachments can be downloaded from the item. Delete homework only when it was posted in error.

### 6.7 Term summaries

Open **Term Summary**, select the student and term context, enter the required summary information, and save. Confirm that the correct student and term are selected before writing a comment.

### 6.8 Schemes of work

To create a scheme:

1. Open **Schemes** and select **Create**.
2. Choose the academic context, subject, and class.
3. Save the scheme.
4. Add topics manually or use the text import.
5. Arrange and save the plan.
6. Add or review subtopics.
7. Update item and subtopic progress as teaching proceeds.
8. Select **Submit** when the scheme is ready for review.

After submission, monitor the status. If changes are requested, open the scheme, read the feedback, revise it, and resubmit.

Teachers assigned as Heads of Department can open the **HOD Schemes** area to review departmental schemes, approve them, or request changes.

### 6.9 Requisitions

1. Open **Requisitions**.
2. Select **Create Requisition**.
3. Add the required item(s), quantities, purpose, and other details.
4. Submit the request.
5. Open the requisition later to monitor its status.

A pending request may be cancelled where the system allows it. Once processing has begun, contact the inventory or office team if a correction is required.

Use **Events** to create and manage calendar events and comments. Confirm date, time, audience, and description before publication.

## 9. Parent/Guardian web guide

### 9.2 Marks and report cards

1. Open **Children’s Marks**.
2. Select the child.

### 9.4 Events and announcements

3. Choose the relevant child or category if requested.

## 10. Accounts Officer guide

### 10.1 Student results access

Open **Students** to review fee-related access status. Search or filter the list, then block or unblock a student as authorised. Bulk controls can apply the same action to selected students.

A fee block restricts result-related pages; it should be applied and removed according to approved school policy.

### 10.2 Import fee balances

1. Open **Fees** and select **Import**.
2. Upload the supported spreadsheet.
3. Review the generated preview and batch details.
4. Check matched, unmatched, and invalid rows.
5. Manually match a row where the system provides that option.
6. Correct the source file or unresolved data as needed.
7. Confirm the batch only when the preview is accurate.
8. Open the completed batch to review the result.

Never confirm an import without reviewing totals and unmatched students.

### 10.3 Reports

Use **Exam Summaries** and **Reports** to filter, preview, and download individual or bulk PDFs. Confirm the academic year, term, and class before exporting.

## 11. Librarian guide

### 11.1 Manage books and copies

Open **Books** to search the catalogue, create a title, edit details, or remove an incorrect record.

To add a book:

1. Select **Add Book**.
2. Enter the title, author, ISBN, category, and other available details.
3. Use ISBN lookup where available to retrieve book details, then verify them.
4. Save the title.
5. Add physical copies and their unique copy identifiers.

The book title and each physical copy are separate records. Add a copy for every item that can be issued.

### 11.2 Import books

1. Open the book import page.
2. Upload the supported file.
3. Review the preview.
4. Correct invalid or duplicate information.
5. Apply the import.

### 11.3 Issue a book

1. Open **Borrowings** and select the issue option.
2. Identify the student or teacher.
3. Scan or enter the book-copy identifier.
4. Confirm the book and borrower.
5. Enter the issue and due dates if requested.
6. Complete the issue.

### 11.4 Return and overdue processing

To return a book, open the return option, identify the issued copy, verify the borrower and title, and confirm the return.

Use **Mark Overdue** to update eligible loans after checking their due dates. Resolve incorrect or damaged-item situations according to library policy.

## 17. Statuses and access controls

Common statuses include:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | Available for current use |
| Inactive | Retained but not currently available |
| Draft | Still being prepared |
| Submitted | Sent for review |
| Approved | Accepted by an authorised reviewer |
| Changes requested | Must be revised and resubmitted |
| Locked | Editing is restricted |
| Finalized | The period or record is considered complete |
| Pending | Waiting for action |
| Completed/Fulfilled | Processing has finished |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn before completion |
| Overdue | A due date has passed without completion/return |
| Results blocked | Academic results are restricted because of the configured fee-access control |

## 18. Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

- Confirm the email spelling and password.
- Check that Caps Lock is off.
- Use **Forgot your password?**
- Ask the administrator whether the account is active.

### I signed in but cannot see a module

Your role may not have access. Teachers also need valid class/subject assignments. Contact the administrator with the exact missing option.

### A teacher cannot see a class or subject

The administrator should verify the active academic year, class-subject assignment, teacher assignment, and student-subject assignment.

### Marks or reports are unavailable

Check the selected academic year and term. The results may not have been released, the term may be locked, or a fee-related block may apply. Contact the appropriate school office.

### The timetable is empty or outdated

Confirm that the administrator published a template and that the user is assigned to its entries. Refresh the browser or mobile app.

### A parent cannot register

Confirm that the code contains exactly 10 letters/numbers and is no older than seven days. If it is expired or used, ask the school to reprint the login slip or link the child to the existing account.

### An import has errors

Do not confirm it. Review the preview, correct the spreadsheet format or values, resolve unmatched students/books, and upload again.

### An attachment will not open

Confirm internet access, retry the download, and use an application that supports the file type. Ask the sender to replace a corrupt or unsupported file.

### The mobile app shows old information

Reconnect to the internet and select **Refresh**. Sign out and in again only if refreshing does not resolve the issue.

### I made an incorrect change

Stop further processing and contact the responsible administrator. Provide the record name, date/time, and action taken. Do not create duplicate records to hide an error.

## 19. Security and data protection

- Use an individual account; never share credentials.
- Use a unique password and change temporary passwords immediately.
- Sign out of shared devices.
- Verify recipients and audiences before sending messages or announcements.
- Download student records only for authorised school work.
- Store exported PDFs, CSV files, and spreadsheets securely.
- Do not send sensitive student information through personal messaging services.
- Report suspected unauthorised access promptly.
- Deactivate former staff accounts without deleting required historical records.

---

## 20. Getting support

When reporting a problem, provide:

1. Your name and account role.
2. The page or app screen involved.
3. What you were trying to do.
4. The exact error message.
5. The academic year, term, class, or student involved, where appropriate.
6. A screenshot that does not expose unnecessary sensitive information.
7. The date and approximate time of the problem.

Contact the school’s designated system administrator or support channel. Do not include your password in a support request.
